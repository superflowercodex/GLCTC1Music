MODULE 02 · upload.html

♫ MUSIC — PLAY SPACE · COSMIC 1200 DECKS

Twin DJ decks, a two-channel mixer with headphone cue, the GLCTC1 Master Library, Playlist Master, Favourites and Banks A–Z.

OPEN MUSIC ›
Deck A & Deck B🔒 Editing tracks, favourites & playlist syncMIDI controller support

EACH DECK (A & B)

Platter / jog

Touch and hold the spinning platter to stop the record; drag it to scratch or nudge. The scratch ribbon under the platter shows the playhead and can be dragged to move through the track.

Play Stop

Start and stop the deck.

Cue

Press once to set a cue point at the current position (the deck pauses there). Press again to clear the cue point.

CUE/PLAY

Jumps back to your cue point and plays from it. Press it repeatedly to re-trigger the same spot — great for drops and stutters.

◀ SNAP SNAP ▶

The deck finds the first kick, works out the BPM and builds a bar-by-bar beat grid. SNAP ▶ jumps forward one bar on that grid, ◀ SNAP jumps back. Works whether or not the deck is playing or cued.

Pitch

Slide to speed the track up or down to match tempos. Double-tap the fader (or use its reset) to return to 0.0%.

SL MO

Slow-mo: drops the deck to 50% speed for a dramatic slow-down effect. Press again to return.

Tap Tempo

Tap in time with the beat to set the deck speed to your tapped tempo. Hold the button for about 1 second to cancel and return to normal speed.

Gain · Pan

Gain sets the deck's input level — keep it out of the red. Pan moves the deck left/right in the stereo field.

High · Low · Filter

Two-band EQ to cut or boost highs and lows, plus a sweepable filter for build-ups and transitions.

Reverb

Adds space to the deck. Use small amounts so the mix stays clear.

MIXER & HEADPHONES

Channel 1 / Channel 2

Volume fader for each deck with a CLIP light. If CLIP lights, bring Gain or the fader down.

Crossfader

Slides the mix between Deck A (left) and Deck B (right).

Master volume

Final output level for the whole mix.

🎧 Headphone cue

Press 🎧 CUE A / 🎧 CUE B to listen to a deck in your headphones before the crowd hears it. Headphone Gain A/B set each level and CUE MIX blends between A ONLY and B ONLY. Needs a browser and device that support a separate headphone output.

GLCTC1 MASTER LIBRARY

Search

Type in Search library… and choose a field (All Fields, Artist, Track Title, Album, Genre, BPM, Key, Frequency, Chakra, Planet, Spatial, Notes and more). Tap a column heading to sort.

Load & preview

Select a track, then Load Deck A, Load Deck B or Add to Bank. The mini player has Prev, Next, Shuffle and Share Track.

Track data

Each record can hold Master Audio, Stem 1–4, Artist, Track Title, Album, Genre, BPM, Key, Frequency (Hz), Chakra, Planet, Spatial, Notes, ID Cat No, Copyright and Cover Art.

✏️ Edit your tracks 🔒

Track editing lives in your own space now: ♛ ARTIST → ♫ MUSIC. Press ✎ on a track to change its details, replace the master, stems or cover, or 🗑 delete it.

Add Track to Library

Takes you to Audio Analyse to analyse and upload a new track.

🎵 PLAYLIST MASTER & ♡ FAVOURITES

Create a playlist

Type a name in ENTER PLAYLIST NAME and press ➕ CREATE PLAYLIST. Pick it in the Playlist Library to select it.

Fill it

Select a Master Library track, then ➕ ADD TRACK TO PLAYLIST. 💿 CREATE ALBUM PLAYLIST builds a playlist from a whole album.

Play it

The playlist player has Shuffle, Previous, Play/Pause, Next and Repeat. Send the current track to a deck with 🎚️ LOAD TO DECK A / B.

Tidy up

🗑️ DELETE TRACK FROM PLAYLIST removes one track; 🗑️ DELETE PLAYLIST removes the selected playlist.

Back up / restore

⬇ BACKUP saves all your playlists to a file; ⬆ RESTORE loads them back. The ☁ status shows whether your playlists are synced to your account.

Share

📤 SHARE TRACK and 📋 SHARE PLAYLIST share what you're playing.

♡ My Favourite Tracks 🔒

Tap the heart on any track to save it. ▶ PLAY FAVOURITES plays them all; ＋ ADD FAVOURITES TO SELECTED PLAYLIST copies them into a playlist. ↻ REFRESH reloads the list.

🏦 BANKS A–Z

What banks are

26 crates (A to Z) for organising tracks before a set — e.g. A = warm-up, B = peak time.

Put tracks in a bank

From the library use Add to Bank, or in Playlist Master pick a letter in SELECT BANK A – Z and press 🏦 ADD PLAYLIST TO BANK to add the selected playlist track.

Use a bank

Tap a letter button to open that bank, then load its tracks to Deck A or Deck B.

📡 SEND BANKS TO LIVE

Copies all 26 banks to the decks on the ♙ LIVE page so your prepared crates are ready for a live set. This works in the same browser on the same device.

🎛️ HARDWARE CONTROLLERS

Plug-and-play MIDI

Connect a MIDI DJ controller before opening the page (Chrome, Edge or Opera). It is detected automatically. The Vestax VCI-300 is mapped: jog wheels, channel volumes, pitch, gain, high, low, filter, crossfader, cue mix, Cue, Play, Slow-mo, Tap/Sync and CUE/PLAY for both decks.

DJ WORKFLOW
  1. Build a playlist or fill a Bank with the tracks for your set.
  2. Load Deck A, then Deck B.
  3. Set Gain on each deck so neither CLIP light flashes.
  4. Pre-listen to the next deck with 🎧 CUE and match its speed with Pitch or Tap Tempo.
  5. Set a Cue point on the first beat (SNAP helps you land on a bar).
  6. Press CUE/PLAY on the beat, then blend with the EQ, filter and crossfader.

PROJECT, TRANSPORT & EXPORT

Project

Name your project, then Save, Open Saved or Clear Arrangement. Projects live in this browser — use Export Project Package to back up or move to another device.

Export

Save Locally, Export Mix (WAV) or Download Project WAV (renders MIDI, audio clips and video audio).

Transport

|◀ go to start · ▶ play/pause · ■ stop · ⟲ loop · ● record from microphone.

MODULES ON

When on, the master transport also starts every module set to PLAY WITH MASTER. Turn it off to play the timeline only.

▶ PLAY ALL MODULES

Starts every module together in time. Press again to stop them all. ↺ RESET ALL MODULES returns everything to factory / demo song.

Tempo & grid

Set BPM, time signature (4/4, 3/4, 6/8, 5/4), CLICK metronome and Snap (1/4–1/32). The counters show BAR:BEAT:TICK and MIN:SEC:MS. The master BPM drives every module.

🎹 MIDI LEARN

Arm MIDI Learn, touch an on-screen control, then move a knob or press a pad on your hardware to link them.

SOUND & MEDIA BROWSER

AUDIO

＋ Import Audio for loops, samples and recordings — BPM and key are detected automatically (also from MP4 video audio). Add Synth or Audio tracks here.

MIDI

＋ Add MIDI Track, then Open Piano Roll to draw notes. Tap cells to add/remove notes; each row is a pitch.

VIDEO · IMAGE

Import MP4/WebM video or PNG/JPG/GIF/WebP/SVG pictures, put them on a video track and they show on the visual stage for the length of the clip.

PROJECT

New Project, Open Project File and Export Project Package.

PLUGINS

Built-in instruments and effects always work. Web Audio Module (WAM) plugins load from a file or URL. Native VST/AU files cannot run inside a browser. Enable MIDI Input connects a keyboard.

ARRANGEMENT / TIMELINE & OBJECT EDITOR

Tracks

＋ Audio Track, ＋ Video Track, ＋ FX Automation and ＋ FX Object. Swipe or scroll sideways to move along the timeline; − / ＋ zoom.

Edit a clip

Select a clip to open the Object Editor: name, start and length in bars, Cut / Copy / Paste at playhead, Delete, Split at Playhead, Duplicate, Trim to Playhead, Fade In/Out, Cut Out Selection, Keep Selection and Clip gain. Drag the ◀ ▶ handles to set clip start and end.

Tempo & pitch per clip

Shows the clip's BPM and key. Set project BPM copies it to the project; ½× / 2× fix half/double readings. Pitch (semitones), Fine tune (cents), Sync tempo to project BPM and Key lock.

Automation

In TRACK MIXER · FX pick an FX target, then draw by hand or choose a shape: Flat, Ramp up/down, Triangle, Sine LFO or Saw.

THE MODULES

SAMPLER / LOOPER PADS

Record (● REC) or Load sample into pads. Pad modes: Toggle loop, One-shot, Gate (hold). Loop length 1–8 bars, Loop, Reverse, ½× / 2×, Fit loop to project tempo and Bounce → Timeline. ▶ PAD auditions; ▶ PLAY PATTERN plays the synced loop.

GLCTC1 80LA8 — drum machine

16-step sequencer with patterns A Main · B Variation · C Break · D Fill. Tap a step to toggle; select a step to set its velocity. Swing, Euclidean Fill, Randomise and live tap pads. Every drum has its own level, pan, pitch, decay, tone, overdrive, delay and reverb. Key Lock keeps tuning fixed when tempo changes.

GLCTC1 DIVINE09 — hybrid drum lab

8-bar patterns (A Club Driver · B Warehouse · C Broken Beat · D Fill/Chaos) with 12 kits from 909 punch and 808 deep to Glitch and Hybrid. Randomise, Clear, Copy Bar → Next, Euclidean Fill, Half-/Double-time, Reverse Bar, Save/Load Kit JSON. Voice Designer, four extra sample channels, live pads and master Drive, Room, Hat tone, Bitcrush, Filter sweep and Stutter.

GLCTC1 30LEE — acid bass

Mono resonant acid bass. 16-step sequencer: tap to toggle, Shift-tap = accent, right-click = slide. Scale and octave, ⚡ ACID GENERATOR, tempo-synced arpeggiator (Up, Down, Up/Down, Pattern, Random), live keyboard note entry, Devil Fish-style mods and an FX destruction bus (Stutter, Filter Freeze, Tape Drop, Panic). BOUNCE → TIMELINE prints it to audio.

GLCTC1 0010110 SYNTH

SH-101-inspired mono synth with a 16-step pattern (Shift-tap = accent), root note and ⚡ GENERATE. Play the keyboard with computer keys A–L; HOLD, ARP and LATCH. Record the audio (● REC AUDIO) or the MIDI notes (◎ SEND MIDI). Layer Lab stacks oscillators and a sample layer; Glitch FX: Stutter, Freeze, Tape Stop, Reverse Sample.

GALACTIC ONE — polysynth

Jupiter-8-inspired dual-patch polysynth (Patch A / B, Whole · Split · Dual keys) with presets like Galactic Massive, Jupiter Strings, Supernova Lead and Wobble Bass. Step sequencer/arp with 8 patterns up to 32 steps (tap = on/off, drag bars to draw). Dual keyboards: touch lower on a key for more velocity, slide for glissando; PC keys Z-row = lower, Q-row = upper. Double-tap a knob to reset, Shift-drag for fine. ● REC MIDI, SEND PATTERN → MIDI, PANIC.

GALACTIC VOX — vocal engine

Turn on the mic (🎤 MIC OFF) or load a vocal file. Vocal types from Natural and Male→Female to Robot, Alien, Choir of Me, Trap Autotune, Vocoder Choir and Ghost, plus a tuner. Singing sequencer and keyboard (drop audio files on a key to map samples; PC keys Q-row), piano + multi-sampler and 16 pads you can load or record, each set to loop.

MASTER MIXER

One channel per track sending to master, with track FX (Filter, Delay, Reverb), delay send and insert FX (compressor, 3-band EQ, low/high-pass). Master FX, master volume with CLIP (tap to reset), headphone and booth outputs with Detect output devices.

GLCTC1 WORKSTATION · MASTER CONSOLE

PAD-64

Pick a sound pad, then paint its 64 steps (4 bars of 16ths). Load a factory or genre preset (Techno, House, DnB…) or LOAD DEMO PATTERN. Every pad runs through the PAD-64 channel so you can mute, solo, meter and record it.

WAVE & SAMPLE LAB

◉ WAVE keeps up to 2 minutes of recent audio — drag the wave to grab a section, then Select All, Trim, Cut, Undo and SAVE TO PAD (banks A–D × 16 pads) or SAVE WAV. Load from ☁ Master Library or your device. Lo-fi tools: bit depth (24 to 4-bit), sample rate (down to 8 kHz, incl. 26 kHz SP-1200), pitch ±12 st, Normalize, ±3 dB, Fades, Trim Silence, export WAV 16/24/32-bit.

✂ STEM SPLITTER

Import a file or load a Master Library track, pick isolation (Normal, Tight, Max, Cleanest) and press ✂ SPLIT 4 STEMS → Vocals · Drums · Bass · Other.

ANALOG MIXER

ARM a channel to play it from the keyboard (arm several to layer). AUD, BYP, faders and pans, LIMITER, PLAY ALL / STOP ALL, MUTE / UNMUTE ALL, SOLO SELECTED, CLEAR SOLO.

LIVE PERFORMANCE

Arm any of the 8 modules (Looper, 80LA8, DIVINE09, 30LEE, 0010110, Galactic One, Galactic Vox, PAD-64) to play them from the master keyboard; ARM ALL / DISARM ALL; CHAIN modules. 8 assignable knobs, 16 assignable pads in 4 banks and 2 ribbons (master volume, pitch bend, mod wheel, module macro, play/stop, panic, hold, octave…).

MASTER KEY 37

37 keys (25 on phones) with velocity, pitch and mod wheels. OCT − / OCT +, TOUCH VEL, BEND RANGE, FX ON/OFF, IMPORT AUDIO, EXPORT MIX WAV and SCENES (snapshot the whole console). The TARGET line shows which modules the keys play.

✦ SONG COMPOSER

Choose a section and preset for each module to build one song from every module. Tempo is read-only — the DAW master BPM is the only tempo control. Its STOP halts every module and returns the timeline to bar 1. A demo song is ready: press ♪ PLAY SONG.

ACTION SEQUENCER / RECORDED SPACE

ARM ACTIONS → ● RECORD TAKE → perform → ■ STOP / FINISH. Instrument and mixer moves become a beat-aligned, editable lane. NEW LAYER, PLAY ARRANGEMENT, Export/Import Take JSON, Clear.

GALACTIC VISUAL STAGE

Previews video and image clips in time with the playhead. Switch ▭ Widescreen 16:9, ▯ Full Phone 9:16 or ⛶ Fullscreen.

MAKE YOUR FIRST BEAT
  1. Set the BPM at the top (e.g. 130).
  2. Open ◉ DRUM CREATE, tap steps on the 80LA8 or pick a DIVINE09 kit and pattern.
  3. Open ∿ SYNTHS, press ⚡ ACID GENERATOR on the 30LEE for a bassline.
  4. Press ▶ PLAY ALL MODULES and balance levels in ⫶ MODULE MIX.
  5. Use BOUNCE → TIMELINE to print parts to audio, arrange them in ▤ SEQUENCE.
  6. Save the project, then Export Mix (WAV).

MODULE 06 · audio-analyse.html

∿ AUDIO ANALYSE

Analyse a track against the GLCTC1 frequency system and upload it to the Master Library.

OPEN ANALYSE ›
🔒 Upload needs sign-in⚡ Complete Check is admin only

1 · Audio input

Drop an audio file on DROP AUDIO HERE or tap to choose one.

2 · ⚡ Analyse audio

Produces Genre, BPM, Key, Time signature, Frequency (Hz), Chakra and Planet.

3 · Fix the tempo if needed

👆 TAP TEMPO along to the beat, then USE TAPPED BPM (or RESET).

4 · Metadata

Complete Track Title, Album (optional), Copyright and Notes. Artist is your signed-in artist name.

5 · ✔ I own the rights

Tick I OWN THE RIGHTS TO THIS TRACK. Only music you wrote, produced or fully own (including samples and vocals) can be uploaded, and you agree it can be played and remixed on GLCTC1. Upload won't work without it.

6 · 🚀 Upload to Master Library

Your audio is analysed in its original format, then converted in your browser to a high-quality 320 kbps MP3 before upload, so large tracks fit the storage limit.

⚡ Master Library Complete Check

Admin tool: one scan of every track with the same analyser used for uploads — tempo (it knows reggae, dub and hip hop half-time from drum & bass / jungle double-time), key, sacred frequency · chakra · planet and genre. Every suggested change has its own tick; 🎧 listen and 👆 tap the tempo on any track; 💾 SAVE once; ↩ UNDO restores the previous values. Genres in the same family as the artist's choice are kept, and frequencies are only changed when a clear tone is found.

UPLOAD A TRACK
  1. Sign in.
  2. Drop your audio file.
  3. ⚡ ANALYSE AUDIO and check the BPM.
  4. Fill in the title, copyright and notes.
  5. Tick ✔ I OWN THE RIGHTS TO THIS TRACK.
  6. 🚀 UPLOAD TO MASTER LIBRARY — it's now on the decks, radio and records.

MODULE 17

TROUBLESHOOTING

No sound

Browsers only play audio after you tap something. Press Play once, check the volume, master fader and crossfader, and make sure your phone isn't on silent.

A button says sign in

Log in on Home first, then come back. Saving, posting, chat and going live all need an account.

My project / playlist is missing

Items marked 💾 are saved in that browser on that device. Use DAW Export Project Package or Playlist ⬇ BACKUP to move them.

Banks didn't reach Live

📡 SEND BANKS TO LIVE works within the same browser on the same device — send from Music, then open Live there.

Video won't play

The provider may block embedding — use OPEN VIDEO to watch it on their site.

Wrong BPM

Use ½× / 2× or Tap Tempo (Analyse, Video, DAW). Drum & bass is often read at half speed.

MIDI controller not found

Use Chrome, Edge or Opera, connect the controller, then reload the page and allow MIDI access.

“This can't be posted”

The GLCTC1 Guard found racism, hate, porn or abuse in your text, link or file name. Swearing is fine — change the blocked part and send again.

Photo or video blocked

The scanner detected nudity. If it says the scanner couldn't run, check your connection and try again — every photo and video must be checked before it can be posted.

DAW feels slow on a phone

Use the 📱 POCKET room, or a single room instead of FULL STUDIO.
