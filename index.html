WHAT IS GLCTC1 MUSIC?

GLCTC1 Music is a free, all-in-one Galactic music space for independent artists, DJs, producers, creators and anyone who loves music. DJ decks, a full studio, remixing, music videos, live streaming, radio, social and bookings — the jobs of a dozen apps in one place. Everything runs in your web browser on phone, tablet or computer, with nothing to install.

DJ online

Mix on the Cosmic 1200 Decks: two decks with cue, pitch, tap tempo, EQ, filter, reverb, headphone cue, crossfader, playlists, Banks A–Z and MIDI controller support.

Make beats in your browser

The GLCTC1 DAW has drum machines, an acid bass synth, polysynths, a vocal engine, a looper, a four-stem splitter, a song composer and WAV export.

Analyse & upload your music

Audio Analyse finds BPM, key, genre and its GLCTC1 frequency, chakra and planet, then adds your track to the Master Library.

Remix any song

Every track in the GLCTC1 Master Library can be pulled into the studio, split into vocals, drums, bass and other parts, and turned into something new.

Make a music video

In Video Space a 3D character dances and sings to your song on the beat. Render it as an MP4 to post anywhere.

Go live & listen

Stream and chat in Live Space, tune in to GLCTC1 Radio and see the Top 40 records.

Grow as an artist

Build your artist profile, post stories, share on the Open Space board and take bookings with your own availability calendar.
