MODULE 02

MUSIC — COSMIC 1200 DECKS

The GLCTC1 DJ, playback, library and playlist environment.

DECK A & DECK B

Two independent decks let you load, prepare and play tracks for mixing.

TRANSPORT

PLAY STOP CUE CUE / PLAY Control playback and cueing of the selected deck.

PITCH

Adjust playback speed so tracks can be brought closer together in tempo for mixing.

SNAP

Use SNAP as the deck timing-assist control when preparing beat-aligned playback.

TAP TEMPO

Tap in time with the music to establish a working tempo for the deck.

GAIN & PAN

GAIN controls deck level. PAN controls left/right stereo placement. Keep gain clean and avoid clipping.

HIGH & LOW

Use the channel EQ to shape high and low frequency content while mixing.

FILTER

Shape the frequency range of a deck for transitions and creative movement.

REVERB

Add spatial ambience to a deck signal. Use it carefully so the mix stays clear.

HEADPHONE CUE

Monitor a channel before bringing it into the main mix. Use channel cue selection and CUE MIX to balance headphone monitoring.

CROSS-FADER

Move the mix between the two deck channels for transitions between Deck A and Deck B.

MASTER LIBRARY

Search and select the central GLCTC1 music catalogue, then load tracks to the decks. Records can contain artwork, BPM, key, frequency, chakra, planet and spatial information.

BANKS A–Z

Banks are organised playlist spaces. Arrange tracks within a bank and use the bank as a prepared collection.

PLAYLIST MASTER

Create playlists, add/remove tracks, load tracks to Deck A or B, add playlists to Banks, and organise material before a set.

TRACK MANAGEMENT

Master Library records can include Master Audio, Stem 1–4, Artist, Track Title, Album, Genre, BPM, Key, Frequency, Chakra, Planet, Spatial, Notes, ID Cat No, Copyright and Cover Art.

SHUFFLE & SET PREPARATION

Use shuffle when you want random track selection. For a controlled set, arrange a playlist or Bank first and load tracks deliberately.

DJ WORKFLOW: Build or choose a playlist → organise a Bank → load Deck A and Deck B → set gain and cue → prepare pitch/tempo → use EQ/filter and crossfader to perform.

MODULE 04

RADIO SPACE

The shared GLCTC1 station for continuous listening, visuals and live interaction.

RADIO NOW PLAYING

Shows the current station track and available information such as artwork, BPM, key, frequency, chakra, planet and spatial data.

CONTINUOUS PLAYBACK

The station is designed to continue playing. When a track finishes, the station advances rather than requiring every listener to choose another song.

SHARED STATION CLOCK

A shared timing state lets browsers follow the same station track and playback position rather than creating separate listener streams.

PLAYER CONTROLS

The player provides the available Previous, Play, Next, Shuffle and Volume controls according to station permissions.

FREQUENCY VISUALISER

Audio-reactive visual material responds to the station's musical/frequency information.

VIDEO

Paste a supported video/live URL, use LOAD VIDEO, or clear the current video.

WEBCAM

Use the webcam control for the live video input in the Radio Space live experience when authorised.

CHAT

The Radio Space chat provides live communication for listeners and participants.

RADIO LEVEL

Controls the station audio level within the available mixer.

LIVE ARTIST LEVEL

Controls the live artist signal level when live audio is active.

DUCKING

Ducking lowers the radio signal while live speech or another live signal needs priority. Duck amount controls the reduction.

RECOVERY & MASTER

Recovery controls how radio returns after ducking. Master controls final mixer output level.

RADIO CONCEPT: Public listeners follow the shared station. Authorised controls are separate from ordinary listener playback so the station remains one continuous GLCTC1 broadcast experience.

MODULE 05

AUDIO ANALYSE

Analyse a track against the GLCTC1 frequency system and prepare it for the Master Library.

1 — AUDIO INPUT

Drop an audio file into AUDIO INPUT or tap to select a file.

2 — ANALYSE AUDIO

Press ⚡ ANALYSE AUDIO to analyse the selected audio.

GENRE

Review the genre information and use it as Master Library metadata.

BPM

BPM identifies tempo in beats per minute and supports mixing and track preparation.

KEY

Review the musical key information produced by the analysis.

FREQUENCY

Frequency provides the GLCTC1 frequency information used by the chakra/planet system.

CHAKRA & PLANET

These fields connect the selected GLCTC1 frequency with its chakra and planetary association.

MASTER LIBRARY METADATA

Complete Track Title, Artist, Album, Genre, BPM, Key, Frequency, Chakra, Planet, Copyright and Notes. Artist is the signed-in artist.

UPLOAD TO MASTER LIBRARY

When the metadata is ready, use 🚀 UPLOAD TO MASTER LIBRARY to submit the track.

AUDIO CONVERSION

The original audio is analysed in its original format. On upload, GLCTC1 converts it in the browser to a high-quality 320 kbps MP3 before sending it to Supabase.
