MODULE 02

MUSIC — COSMIC 1200 DECKS

The main GLCTC1 music workspace, DJ system, Master Library, Banks A–Z and Playlist Master.

What Music Does

The Music page is the main GLCTC1 production and playback environment.

It combines the two-deck Cosmic 1200 DJ system with the Master Library, playlist banks, playlist management and track organisation.

The two decks allow tracks to be loaded, played, mixed and manipulated independently.

DECK A & DECK B

GLCTC1 provides two independent decks so that two tracks can be prepared and mixed together.

DECK A

The left-side deck. Load a track from the Master Library or another available track source and control its playback independently.

DECK B

The right-side deck. It operates independently from Deck A and is used for the second track in a two-deck mix.

TRANSPORT CONTROLS

Control How to use it
PLAY Starts playback of the loaded track.
STOP Stops the deck's playback.
SL MO Use the available SL MO control as provided by the deck interface.
CUE Provides the deck's cue function for preparing playback.
CUE / PLAY Use this combined transport control when preparing or launching a deck from cue.

PITCH

The Pitch control changes the playback speed of the deck.

Use Pitch when matching the speed of one track against another or creating a controlled tempo transition.

Adjust gradually when beatmatching. Large pitch movements will create larger changes in playback speed.

SNAP

SNAP is the beat-alignment function available on the deck.

Use SNAP when you want the deck's playback position to be brought into alignment with the beat timing calculated for the track.

SNAP is intended as a timing/beat-assistance function. It does not replace listening to the tracks and checking the musical transition.

TAP TEMPO

Tap Tempo lets you establish or correct the tempo manually by tapping in time with the music.

Tap repeatedly and consistently with the beat. The system uses the timing between taps to determine the tempo.

GAIN & PAN

GAIN

Controls the level of the individual deck before it reaches the mix. Use it to balance tracks that have different recording levels.

PAN

Controls the stereo position of the deck. Centre keeps the signal centred between left and right.

HIGH & LOW

HIGH

Adjusts the high-frequency content of the deck. Use it for tonal shaping during a mix.

LOW

Adjusts the low-frequency content of the deck. This can be useful when transitioning between bass-heavy tracks.

FILTER

Filter changes the frequency balance of the deck, allowing you to shape or sweep the sound during a mix.

Use it deliberately. Extreme filter movements can substantially change the character of the track.

REVERB

Reverb adds spatial ambience to the deck's audio.

Use small amounts for subtle space or increase it for a more pronounced effect.

HEADPHONE CUE

The Headphone Cue section is designed for monitoring the channels independently from the main mix.

Use the channel cue controls to choose which deck you want to monitor through the headphone path.

The CUE MIX control determines the balance between the cue signal and the main mix in the headphone monitoring system.

CHANNEL 1 / CHANNEL 2

The mixer provides two channel paths corresponding to the two-deck system.

Use the channel controls to manage the contribution of Deck A and Deck B to the final mix.

CROSSFADER

The Crossfader controls the balance between the two deck channels.

  • Move toward one side to favour one deck.
  • Move toward the opposite side to favour the other deck.
  • Centre the Crossfader for an even contribution from both sides.

MASTER LIBRARY

The Master Library is the central catalogue for GLCTC1 music.

Tracks can contain audio, metadata, artwork and additional production information.

Master Library information can include:

  • Master Audio
  • Stem 1
  • Stem 2
  • Stem 3
  • Stem 4
  • Artist
  • Track Title
  • Album
  • Genre
  • BPM
  • Key
  • Frequency (Hz)
  • Chakra
  • Planet
  • Spatial
  • Notes
  • ID Cat No
  • Copyright
  • Cover Art

SEARCHING THE MASTER LIBRARY

Use the library search field to locate tracks rather than manually searching through the entire catalogue.

Search using information such as track title, artist or other available library information.

MASTER LIBRARY SORTING

The Master Library table can be organised using its column headings where sorting is available.

Select a column heading to change the ordering of the displayed tracks alphabetically or numerically, depending on the type of information in that column.

SELECTING A TRACK

Select a track from the Master Library to work with its available information and deck functions.

The selected-track system is also used by the track-management controls.

TRACK MANAGEMENT

UPDATE MASTER LIBRARY TRACK

Use the update function after editing the selected track's library information.

Check the fields carefully before saving so the catalogue remains complete and organised.

DELETE ALL TRACK DATA

When a track is selected, this function operates on the selected track using the existing protected deletion system.

When no track is selected, the control is used as the library cleanup action for tracks with missing required information.

The cleanup targets tracks where any of the required fields are empty: Master Audio, Artist, Track Title or ID Cat No.

Deletion is destructive. Check the selected track and library information carefully before using a delete function.

BANKS A–Z

Banks A–Z provide 26 separate playlist areas.

Each bank can be used to organise a group of tracks into its own playable collection.

Using Banks

  1. Choose the bank you want to work with.
  2. Add tracks to the selected bank.
  3. Rearrange tracks when required.
  4. Move tracks between banks where supported.
  5. Use the bank's playback functions to play the selected collection.

Bank Naming

Banks can be renamed so they can represent your own sets, genres, projects or other collections.

PLAYLIST MASTER

Playlist Master provides a higher-level way to organise and play playlists.

Create playlists, manage their tracks, load tracks to Deck A or Deck B and send playlist material into the available bank system.

Playlist Functions

  • Create a playlist.
  • Delete a playlist.
  • Add tracks to a playlist.
  • Remove tracks from a playlist.
  • Load a track to Deck A.
  • Load a track to Deck B.
  • Add playlist material to a Bank.
  • Share a track or playlist where the page provides the sharing function.

SHUFFLE & SET PREPARATION

Shuffle can be used when you want the system to select tracks in a random order instead of following the existing sequence.

Use shuffle when exploring the Master Library, preparing a set or creating variation within a collection.

Always check the next track before a live transition when musical continuity matters.

ADDING NEW MUSIC

New music enters the GLCTC1 system through the Audio Analyse workflow.

Use: MUSIC → AUDIO ANALYSE to prepare a new track for the Master Library.

This keeps the library metadata connected to the audio-analysis system.

MODULE 03

LIVE — GLCTC1 SOCIAL

Watch live content, access the Live Artist Pool, use the social space and connect with live performers.

LIVE SPACE

The Live page is the social performance area of GLCTC1.

It brings together live viewing, artists, video, music tools and crowd interaction.

LIVE NOW

The Live Now area provides access to live content currently being presented through the GLCTC1 live system.

LIVE ARTIST POOL

The Live Artist Pool provides access to artists participating in the live system.

Select an artist to access that artist's live viewing route where available.

GO LIVE

The main Social page's GO LIVE control takes the artist to the Artist Live Module.

It does not itself start the broadcast.

The actual broadcast is started from the GO LIVE control inside the Artist Live Module.

Once the artist starts broadcasting, the live control changes to END LIVE.

LOAD VIDEO

The Live page provides a universal video/live URL input.

  1. Paste the video or live URL into the input.
  2. Press LOAD VIDEO.
  3. The page attempts to load the supplied source into the video area.
External websites can prevent their content from being embedded. If a service blocks embedding, GLCTC1 cannot override that restriction.

CROWD CHAT

Crowd Chat allows people in the Social environment to communicate while using the page.

Enter your message and send it to the chat.

Keep the community space respectful and useful.

WATCHING LIVE

When an artist is live, viewers can watch the artist's live performance through the appropriate live viewing area.

The viewer route is associated with the artist being watched so that the correct live session can be received.

MODULE 04

RADIO — RADIO SPACE

The shared GLCTC1 radio station, continuous playback, live video, chat and frequency-reactive visual system.

WHAT RADIO SPACE DOES

Radio Space is the GLCTC1 shared radio environment.

The station uses the GLCTC1 Master Library as its music source and provides a shared listening experience.

CONTINUOUS RADIO PLAYBACK

The radio is designed to continue playing rather than restarting independently for every visitor.

When a track finishes, the station advances to the next track according to the station playback logic.

Shuffle can be used so the station moves through the library in a randomised sequence.

SHARED STATION CLOCK

Radio Space uses the Supabase station timestamp as the shared master clock.

Public browsers repeatedly read the station state and synchronise themselves to the same track and playback position.

This is what allows different listeners to follow the same radio station rather than each browser independently starting the music from the beginning.

NOW PLAYING

Now Playing displays the current station track information.

Depending on the available metadata, this includes:

  • Track title
  • Artist
  • Artwork
  • BPM
  • Key
  • Frequency
  • Chakra
  • Planet
  • Spatial information

RADIO PLAYER

Control Purpose
PREVIOUS Moves to the previous station track where the station controls permit it.
PLAY Starts or resumes radio playback in the browser.
NEXT Advances the station to the next track where authorised.
SHUFFLE Enables randomised track selection for the station playback sequence where supported.
VOLUME Controls the listener's radio playback level.

FREQUENCY VISUALISER

Radio Space contains a real-time visual system that reacts to the audio.

Track frequency and chakra metadata are used as part of the GLCTC1 visual language.

The visualiser is intended to create a living, audio-reactive GLCTC1 environment while the radio plays.

VIDEO

Radio Space includes a video area above the radio player.

Paste a supported video or live URL and select LOAD VIDEO.

Use CLEAR VIDEO to remove the loaded video.

External services may prevent embedding. This is controlled by the external service and cannot be bypassed by GLCTC1.

WEBCAM

The Radio Space webcam control can be used to activate the available camera/video path.

The browser will request the required camera permission.

Camera access must be permitted by the user and browser for the webcam to operate.

FULLSCREEN

Use the Fullscreen control when you want the video or supported visual area to occupy more of the screen.

RADIO SPACE MIXER

The mixer provides control over the radio and live performance audio paths.

RADIO LEVEL

Controls the radio audio level in the mixer.

LIVE ARTIST LEVEL

Controls the contribution of the live artist audio path.

LIVE DUCKING

Allows the radio level to be reduced while live speech/performance audio is active.

DUCKING AMOUNT

Determines how strongly the radio is reduced while ducking is active.

RECOVERY

Controls the return of the radio level after the ducking condition ends.

MASTER

Controls the final master output level.

CHAT

Radio Space includes a chat area for communication around the station.

Enter a message and send it through the chat interface.

MASTER LIBRARY IN RADIO SPACE

Radio Space can access the shared Master Library for station music.

Search and refresh the library using the available controls.

The public-facing station follows the shared station state rather than allowing every visitor to operate the station independently.

MODULE 05

AUDIO ANALYSE

Analyse audio and prepare complete GLCTC1 Master Library metadata before uploading a track.

WHAT AUDIO ANALYSE DOES

Audio Analyse examines an audio file and determines its GLCTC1 musical characteristics.

The page is also the preparation stage for adding new music to the Master Library.

SELECTING AUDIO

  1. Open AUDIO ANALYSE.
  2. Drop an audio file into the audio area or tap the area to select a file.
  3. Wait for the selected file to appear.
  4. Select ANALYSE AUDIO.

GLCTC1 AUDIO META

The analysis produces the musical metadata used by the GLCTC1 system.

  • Genre
  • BPM
  • Key
  • Frequency (Hz)
  • Chakra
  • Planet

MASTER LIBRARY METADATA

Before uploading, complete the Master Library information for the track.

  • Track Title — the name of the track.
  • Artist — populated for the signed-in artist.
  • Album — optional album information.
  • Genre — the track genre.
  • BPM — beats per minute.
  • Key — musical key.
  • Frequency — GLCTC1 frequency classification.
  • Chakra — associated GLCTC1 chakra.
  • Planet — associated GLCTC1 planetary classification.
  • Copyright — copyright information.
  • Notes — additional track information.

MASTER LIBRARY AUDIO PREPARATION

The original audio is analysed in its original format.

When the track is uploaded to the Master Library, GLCTC1 converts it in the browser to a high-quality 320 kbps MP3 before sending it to Supabase.

This reduces the upload file size so larger tracks can stay within the current website storage limit.

The conversion happens for the uploaded Master Library copy. The analysis is performed from the original selected audio.

UPLOAD TO MASTER LIBRARY

After the analysis and metadata are complete, select UPLOAD TO MASTER LIBRARY.

The track is prepared for the Master Library and uploaded using the signed-in artist account.

Manage your GLCTC1 artist identity and account information.

WHAT ARTIST DOES

The Artist page is your account/profile area inside GLCTC1 Music.

Your artist information is associated with your signed-in GLCTC1 account.

ARTIST NAME

ACCOUNT EMAIL

MODULE 08

BOOKING — BOOKING SPACE

Artist availability, booking requests and artist enquiries.

WHAT BOOKING SPACE DOES

Booking Space connects clients with GLCTC1 artists through artist identification, availability and booking requests.

FIND AN ARTIST

Search for an artist using the available artist search fields.

Artist identification can use the artist's name or GLCTC1 User ID.

BOOKING CALENDAR

The calendar displays artist availability using three states:

  • BLACK / UNSET — no availability status has been set.
  • NEON GREEN / AVAILABLE — the artist has marked the date available.
  • DULL GREEN / BOOKED — the date has been marked booked.

ARTIST CALENDAR

A signed-in artist can maintain their own private booking diary/reference calendar.

Tap a calendar date to cycle the date status:

  1. BLACK / UNSET
  2. NEON GREEN / AVAILABLE
  3. DULL GREEN / BOOKED
  4. BLACK / UNSET again

Changes are saved when the artist presses SAVE CALENDAR.

SELECT DATE

When requesting a booking with an artist, select one of the artist's available dates.

Dates that are not available cannot be selected for the booking request.

BOOKING REQUEST

Complete the booking information for the event.

  • Date
  • Event Name
  • Event Type
  • Venue
  • Town / City
  • Country
  • Event Time
  • Contact Name
  • Email
  • Phone
  • Message / Requirements

Check the information before selecting SEND BOOKING REQUEST.

CHAT ID

Where provided by the booking interface, the Chat ID identifies the relevant communication route.

OTHER ARTIST INQUIRY

The Other Artist Inquiry area allows communication with another artist without creating a standard booking request.

Select the artist, enter a subject and write the message before sending the inquiry.

IMPORTANT BOOKING RULE

An artist cannot book themselves through the normal booking request route.

To make a booking request, search for another artist and use that artist's available calendar.

QUICK REFERENCE

The simplest route through GLCTC1 Music.

I WANT TO LISTEN

  1. Open MUSIC to use the Cosmic 1200 Decks and Master Library.
  2. Open RADIO for the shared continuous radio station.
  3. Open LIVE to watch available live performances.

I WANT TO ADD MUSIC

  1. Sign into GLCTC1.
  2. Open AUDIO ANALYSE.
  3. Select your audio file.
  4. Analyse the track.
  5. Check the GLCTC1 metadata.
  6. Complete the Master Library information.
  7. Upload the track to the Master Library.

I WANT TO DJ / MIX

  1. Open MUSIC.
  2. Load a track to Deck A.
  3. Load a second track to Deck B.
  4. Use Pitch and/or Tap Tempo to prepare timing.
  5. Use SNAP where appropriate for beat alignment.
  6. Use Gain and EQ controls to balance the tracks.
  7. Use Headphone Cue to prepare the incoming track.
  8. Use the Crossfader to perform the transition.

I WANT TO GO LIVE

  1. Sign into your GLCTC1 account.
  2. Open LIVE.
  3. Select GO LIVE.
  4. The Artist Live Module opens.
  5. Use the Artist Live Module's GO LIVE button to actually start the broadcast.
  6. When live, the button changes to END LIVE.
  7. Press END LIVE when the broadcast is finished.

I WANT TO BOOK AN ARTIST

  1. Open BOOKING.
  2. Search for the artist by name or GLCTC1 ID.
  3. Check the artist's calendar.
  4. Select an available date.
  5. Complete the event and contact information.
  6. Add your message or requirements.
  7. Send the booking request.

I WANT TO MANAGE MY ARTIST PROFILE

  1. Sign in.
  2. Open ARTIST.
  3. Enter or update your Artist Name.
  4. Check your account information.
  5. Save the Artist Profile.

I WANT TO SHARE SOMETHING

Open OPEN SPACE.

Use the available community functions to share messages, music, ideas, video, audio or files.

Remember that Open Space uses a rolling six-hour active window.

I WANT THE RADIO

Open RADIO.

Radio Space provides the shared station, Now Playing information, continuous playback, video, webcam, chat and frequency-reactive visual experience.
